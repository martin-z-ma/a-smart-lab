Research

Integrated methods for high-impact systems.

We combine engineering knowledge, data and computation to understand complex systems and improve decisions under uncertainty.

01

Methods

AI & Data-Driven Modeling

We develop data-efficient models that accelerate simulation, reveal patterns and support real-time or large-scale analysis without losing sight of physical meaning.

Scientific machine learningSurrogate modelingDeep learningLarge language modelsPredictive analytics

Decisions

Optimisation & Decision Support

Our optimisation frameworks connect technical performance, cost, risk and sustainability objectives. We focus on practical trade-offs, uncertainty and decisions that remain robust across scenarios.

Multi-objective optimisationRobust optimisationNetwork designRisk analysisTechno-economic assessment

Applications

Energy, Infrastructure & CCUS

We study the planning and operation of low-carbon infrastructure, including CO₂ transport and storage networks, hydrogen systems, subsurface processes and integrated energy pathways.

CO₂ transportCCUS infrastructureHydrogen systemsSubsurface modelingDecarbonisation

Applications

Food, Chemical & Biological Process Systems

We translate systems methods into emerging applications in food engineering, process design, biotechnology and human nutrition—where data, mechanisms and decisions are deeply connected.

Food process analyticsProcess systems engineeringBioprocess modelingQuality monitoringSustainable production
